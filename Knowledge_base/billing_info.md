# How to do Billing & Payments

We accept all major credit cards and process payments securely.

## View Your Billing Details
- Go to **Settings > Billing**
- Download invoices
- Update payment method

## Upgrades & Downgrades
You can change your plan at any time. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

## Refund Policy
We offer a 7-day refund window on new purchases. Contact support for help.
