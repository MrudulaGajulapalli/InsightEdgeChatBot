# InsightEdge - Product Overview

InsightEdge is a real-time business analytics platform designed to help teams make smarter decisions, faster. With customizable dashboards, live KPI tracking, and collaborative reporting, InsightEdge empowers teams to act on data instantly.

## Key Features
- Dashboard Builder with drag-and-drop widgets
- Real-time KPI and metric tracking
- Data import from CSV, Google Sheets, or APIs
- Alerts when thresholds are reached
- Team collaboration with access control

## Use Cases
- Sales teams tracking regional performance
- Marketing teams measuring ad campaign ROI
- Executives monitoring company-wide KPIs
- Product managers tracking feature adoption

## Pricing Plans
- Starter (Free): 1 dashboard, 1 data source
- Growth ($29/mo): 5 dashboards, API access
- Premium ($99/mo): Unlimited dashboards, team sharing, SLA
