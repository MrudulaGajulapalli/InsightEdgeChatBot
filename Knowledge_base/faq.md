# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I use InsightEdge without coding knowledge?
Yes! InsightEdge is designed for everyone. You can create dashboards with drag-and-drop tools.

### Q: What data sources can I connect?
You can upload CSV files, connect Google Sheets, or integrate via our REST API.

### Q: Can I try Premium features during the trial?
Yes, all new accounts receive a 7-day free trial of our Premium plan.

### Q: Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use encryption at rest and in transit, and follow industry best practices.
