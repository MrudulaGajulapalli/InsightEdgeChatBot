# Workflow Templates

Speed up your dashboard creation with prebuilt templates

## Templates Available
- **Sales Dashboard**: Leads, revenue, pipeline metrics
- **Marketing Dashboard**: Ad spend, CTR, ROI
- **Product Dashboard**: Feature usage, churn, NPS

To use a template:
1. Go to **Dashboard Templates**
2. Click **“Use Template”**
3. Modify and save your custom version

Templates are a great starting point for new users.
